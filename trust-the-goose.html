What's in the bag02

Log a shot

Club, carry distance, and how far you missed left or right. Ten seconds after each swing — the only thing the app ever asks of you.

My bag

Every club's real average and spread, straight from your shots with a SQL GROUP BY. No guidebook numbers — yours.

Dispersion

Every shot plotted, left/right against carry, one colour per club. Watch your real shot pattern build up over a season.

Confidence tiers

The whole point. The Goose grades its own call on shot count, spread, and whether the target's inside your range — then tells you the truth.

🟢 🟡 🔴

Plays-like weather

Add wind and temperature and the Goose shows what the shot actually plays like before it picks a club. Headwind and cold both cost you yards.

Your data, your machine

Everything lives in one local SQLite file. No account, no cloud, no sample data — the numbers are only ever the shots you logged.

Under the hood03

How the confidence works

For a target distance, the Goose picks the club whose average carry is closest, then grades its own certainty on three honest measures: how many shots you've logged with it, how tight the spread is (sample standard deviation), and whether the target sits inside the range you've actually hit. Ask for 300 when your longest is 250 and it goes red — the Goose won't pretend to know.

Where I'm honest about it

  • The weather model is a rule of thumb — roughly 1% per mph of headwind, a couple of yards per 10°F. Good enough to club up; not launch-monitor physics.
  • It learns from your carries, so the first few rounds it stays cautious (yellow and red) on purpose. Trust is earned once the data's there — that's the feature, not a bug.
