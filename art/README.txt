Put your artwork image files here (PNG, JPG, GIF, or SVG).

Then open gallery.html and, in the PIECES array near the bottom, set each piece's
"img" to its path, for example:  img: "./art/framing-control.png"

Leave "img" empty ("") to show a styled placeholder tile instead.

To make a piece collectible, set its "link" to the marketplace listing URL and
change "status" to "available".
